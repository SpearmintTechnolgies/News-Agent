============================
WRITING RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
============================

- TONE: Direct. Trader-focused. Analytical. No hype, no fluff, no adjectives like "amazing" or "shocking."
- PARAGRAPHS: 1–4 sentences max. Break aggressively. One idea per paragraph.
- BULLET LISTS: 3–4 items only. Used inside H3 sections only. Never in the conclusion or opening.
- INTERNAL LINKS: 2–3 [Read Also: Title](url) links, placed inside H2 body text only. (Invent a highly relevant internal link title if one isn't provided).
- CHART SOURCES: 2–3 mentions of "Source: TradingView" or "Source: CoinGecko" with an image placeholder.
- WORD COUNT: 800–1,200 words of body text.
- SEO KEYWORD PLACEMENT:
  - Primary keyword in H1 (first 5 words)
  - Primary keyword in first 100 words (opening hook)
  - Primary keyword in at least one H2
  - Primary keyword in the Conclusion
  - Secondary keywords in H3 headings and FAQ answers
- STRUCTURE: Always exactly: Hook → H2 Section 1 → H2 Section 2 → H2 Section 3 → H2 Section 4 → Conclusion → FAQs
- HEADINGS: Exactly 1 H1, 4–5 H2s (including Conclusion and FAQs), 2–3 H3s per H2 section. Do not label the hook or introduction with a heading. Do not label sections with bracketed instructions like "[H2]". Output clean markdown headings (e.g. `## Section Title`).
